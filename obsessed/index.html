Obsession #001
The acoustic properties of Victorian sewers
Most people don't know that London's sewer network from the 1860s was engineered with accidental resonance chambers. Certain junctions produce notes. Bazalgette didn't intend it. I've mapped eleven of them.
Started: a wrong turn during a drainage survey
Obsession #002
Why every Cold War bunker smells the same
Concrete, rust, stale air, and something else — a specific plasticky chemical note I've traced back to the insulation used on 1950s electrical cabling. I've been in fourteen bunkers. The smell is identical.
Started: a geography field trip in 1998
Obsession #003
The forgotten women of Ottoman cartography
There are at least six maps held in Istanbul archives that scholars attribute to anonymous female illustrators. Nobody talks about this. The evidence is in the detail style — a particular way of rendering coastlines.
Started: a mistranslated footnote

The idea behind this
Kevin Kelly wrote that you don't need millions of followers. You need true fans — people who genuinely care. This is an attempt to find London's most curious people. One weird obsession at a time.
